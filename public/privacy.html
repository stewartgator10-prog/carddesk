CardDesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

CardDesk is a private tool for managing an eBay trading-card storefront: listings, prices, inventory, orders and profit. It is built and operated by TechBox Solutions. This page explains what CardDesk stores and how it is used.

Who can use CardDesk

CardDesk is not a public service. Only people invited by the account owner can sign in. Each signed-in user can see only their own data.

What we store

  • Your CardDesk login: your email address, used to sign in.
  • eBay connection: when you choose Connect eBay, eBay gives CardDesk access tokens and your eBay user ID and username. The tokens are readable only by CardDesk's servers, never by the browser.
  • Store data from eBay: your listings (title, SKU, price, quantity, photos and links), orders, and fees, so CardDesk can show inventory and profit.
  • Data you enter: inventory, cost basis, watchlist, comps and settings.

How it is used

This data is used only to run CardDesk for you: showing your listings and profit, and making changes on eBay (such as price updates) only when you ask for them. CardDesk does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

Data is stored in a Supabase database and the app is hosted on Netlify. Both are used only as service providers for CardDesk.

Disconnecting and deletion

You can disconnect eBay at any time from the eBay Listings tab, which deletes your saved eBay tokens and listing snapshot from CardDesk. You can also revoke CardDesk's access in your eBay account settings. To delete your CardDesk account and all its data, contact us below.
